In 2017, the iconic Aeron Chair was succeeded by the Remastered version. The production of the Aeron Classic ended, and from 2018 onwards, only refurbished versions were available for purchase. However, it would be incorrect to dismiss the Aeron Classic as it remains a design icon of the 1990s and retains its value, especially when well-maintained. Many collectors still prefer the Aeron Classic due to personal taste. On the other hand, those curious about the new Aeron may wonder if it lives up to its promises. After a thorough seating test, it is evident that the Aeron Remastered represents a leap into the 21st century, incorporating new ergonomic insights and making six small but significant changes.
